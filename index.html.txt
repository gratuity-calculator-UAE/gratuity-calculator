<h1>How to Calculate Gratuity for Resignation and Termination in UAE (2026 Guide)</h1>

<p>
Employees working in the United Arab Emirates often want to understand how their end-of-service benefits are calculated when they leave a job. Whether you resign voluntarily or your employer terminates your contract, gratuity can form an important part of your final settlement. Knowing the latest rules can help you estimate what you may receive and avoid confusion during the exit process.
</p>

<p>
In this guide, we explain how to calculate gratuity for resignation and termination in the UAE, what factors affect your payout, and how you can estimate your amount using a reliable 
<a href="https://gratuity-calculator-uae.ae/">gratuity calculator</a>.
</p>

<h2>What Is Gratuity in the UAE?</h2>

<p>
Gratuity is an end-of-service benefit paid by employers to eligible employees after completing a certain period of employment. It is governed by MOHRE regulations and applies to most private-sector workers in the UAE.
</p>

<p>This amount is generally based on:</p>

<ul>
    <li>Basic salary</li>
    <li>Length of service</li>
    <li>Type of contract</li>
    <li>Reason for leaving (resignation or termination)</li>
</ul>

<p>
Employees can estimate their dues using an online 
<a href="https://gratuity-calculator-uae.ae/">gratuity calculator</a>, which simplifies the process.
</p>

<h2>Is Gratuity Different for Resignation and Termination?</h2>

<p>
Yes. In the UAE, gratuity eligibility usually depends on whether the employee resigns voluntarily or is terminated by the employer.
</p>

<h3>Resignation</h3>
<p>
When an employee chooses to leave the company by submitting resignation, gratuity is usually payable if they have completed at least one year of continuous service.
</p>

<h3>Termination</h3>
<p>
If an employer ends the contract, the employee may still be entitled to full end-of-service benefits, provided the termination is not due to serious misconduct under UAE labour regulations.
</p>

<p>
The legal framework for this is outlined under 
<a href="https://gratuity-calculator-uae.ae/labour-law/">labour law UAE</a>, which explains employee rights in detail.
</p>

<h2>Basic Formula for Gratuity Calculation in UAE</h2>

<p>
The standard gratuity formula in the UAE is based on the employee’s basic salary, excluding allowances such as housing, transport, or bonuses.
</p>

<div class="formula">
Gratuity = (Basic Salary ÷ 30) × Eligible Days
</div>

<p>Eligible days are generally:</p>

<ul>
    <li>21 days’ basic salary per year for the first 5 years</li>
    <li>30 days’ basic salary per year for service beyond 5 years</li>
</ul>

<h2>How to Calculate Gratuity for Resignation</h2>

<p>If you resign from your job, here is the usual process:</p>

<h3>Step 1: Determine Basic Salary</h3>
<p>
Only your monthly basic salary is considered. For example, if your total salary is AED 8,000 but your basic salary is AED 5,000, gratuity will be calculated on AED 5,000.
</p>

<h3>Step 2: Calculate Years of Service</h3>
<p>
The number of complete years and partial months served are included in the final amount.
</p>

<h3>Step 3: Apply Formula</h3>

<p>For example:</p>

<ul>
    <li>Basic salary = AED 5,000</li>
    <li>Service period = 3 years</li>
</ul>

<div class="formula">
Daily salary = 5000 ÷ 30 = 166.67
</div>

<div class="formula">
Gratuity = 166.67 × (21 × 3) = AED 10,500
</div>

<p>So the employee may receive approximately AED 10,500.</p>

<h2>How to Calculate Gratuity for Termination</h2>

<p>
Termination calculations usually follow the same core formula, but the employee may also be entitled to:
</p>

<ul>
    <li>Unpaid salary</li>
    <li>Leave encashment</li>
    <li>Notice period compensation</li>
    <li>Other final settlement dues</li>
</ul>

<p>For instance:</p>

<ul>
    <li>Basic salary = AED 6,000</li>
    <li>Service = 6 years</li>
</ul>

<div class="formula">
First 5 years = (6000 ÷ 30) × (21 × 5) = AED 21,000
</div>

<div class="formula">
6th year = (6000 ÷ 30) × 30 = AED 6,000
</div>

<div class="formula">
Total gratuity = AED 27,000
</div>

<h2>Important Factors That Affect Gratuity</h2>

<h3>1. Type of Employment Contract</h3>
<p>
Contract type can influence resignation terms, notice period, and final settlement. It is important to review your employment documents and your official 
<a href="https://gratuity-calculator-uae.ae/labour-card/">labour card uae</a> details before calculating dues.
</p>

<h3>2. Basic Salary Changes</h3>
<p>
If your basic salary changed during employment, the latest basic salary is typically used.
</p>

<h3>3. Service Less Than One Year</h3>
<p>
Employees who worked for less than one year usually are not entitled to gratuity.
</p>

<h3>4. Misconduct Cases</h3>
<p>
Employees terminated under serious disciplinary violations may lose gratuity entitlement depending on the case.
</p>
